# nomen-dissolve

中文版请见 `README.md`

`nomen-dissolve` is a small page built around a simple idea: sometimes pain becomes lighter when it can finally be named.

What overwhelms people is often not just one event, but a tangled emotional state that feels hard to explain, hard to organize, and hard to face. This page is meant to offer a gentler moment inside that confusion. You write down what you are going through, and the page tries to reflect it back in clearer language.

I have always believed in one sentence: the moment you can name the demon, the demon starts to dissolve.

This project is not trying to hand out perfect answers. It is trying to help people see their inner situation more clearly. Sometimes the pain does not disappear immediately, but once it is described more precisely, it can stop feeling like a shapeless fog.

What this page hopes to do:

- Give people a quiet place to pause and put their current struggle into words.
- Turn vague emotional weight into something more visible, structured, and understandable.
- Be a public-facing page that may genuinely help someone, not just a private experiment.

You can think of it as a small emotional naming tool:

- Write down what is weighing on you.
- Let the page reframe and name that pain.
- Even if it does not solve everything, it may help you understand your own state more clearly.

I chose to keep the page public on GitHub Pages and the repository open because I want it to be used, discovered, and maybe helpful to people beyond my own circle.

If this page helps you put words around something you could not explain before, or helps you feel that your experience is understandable rather than chaotic, that would make me genuinely happy.
